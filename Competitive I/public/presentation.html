Point-in-Time Join Protection

We snapshot usage data daily. When joining to a closed opportunity, we use usage as of the close date—not current state.

LEFT JOIN usage_snapshots us
ON o.account_id = us.account_id
AND us.snapshot_date = o.close_date

Why it matters: Prevents data leakage. We're analyzing usage patterns that existed when the deal closed, not patterns that developed afterward.

Principle: Trust is earned through transparency. Every QA failure is logged. Every join is auditable. The business needs to know the data is right—not just that it exists.

The Join Strategy

Connecting Loss Events
to Usage Signals.

The Core SQL Pattern

SELECT
  o.account_id,
  o.opportunity_id,
  o.competitor_name,
  o.close_date,
  o.arr_at_risk,
  us.usage_score_30d,
  us.usage_trend,
  a.renewal_date,
  DATEDIFF(day, CURRENT_DATE, a.renewal_date) AS days_to_renewal
FROM salesforce.opportunities o
LEFT JOIN analytics.usage_snapshots us
  ON o.account_id = us.account_id
  AND us.snapshot_date = o.close_date  -- Point-in-time
LEFT JOIN salesforce.accounts a
  ON o.account_id = a.account_id
WHERE o.stage = 'Closed Lost'
  AND o.competitor_name IS NOT NULL
  AND a.renewal_date BETWEEN CURRENT_DATE AND CURRENT_DATE + 90;

1
Temporal alignment

Usage snapshot matches opportunity close date—no time travel

2

Both tables keyed on account_id—clean 1:1 relationship

3
Forward-looking filter

Renewal date in next 90 days = intervention window

Deep Dive: Price Sensitive Segment

Price Loss vs
Engagement Loss.

Not all churn is the same. Separating price-driven loss from engagement-driven loss unlocks targeted interventions.

💰 Price-Driven Churn

Characteristics

  • • Usage is moderate or even healthy (30-70%)
  • • "Budget constraints" or "cost reduction" in loss reason
  • • Competitor mentioned is known for lower pricing

Recommended Action

Pricing team: Offer tiered pricing, volume discounts, or multi-year lock-in incentives. They like the product—just need cost justification.

$1.8M

ARR at risk in this segment

📉 Engagement-Driven Churn

Characteristics

  • • Usage is low (<30%) and declining
  • • "Feature gaps" or "better functionality" in loss notes
  • • Competitor offers capabilities we don't have

Recommended Action

Product team: This is a roadmap signal. If 50+ accounts churn citing the same missing feature, that's a product investment priority.

$2.4M

ARR at risk in this segment

How We Flag Price Sensitivity

1. Keyword Detection

NLP scan of opportunity notes for "budget," "cost," "price," "cheaper"

2. Competitor Mapping

If lost to known discount competitor (e.g., "BudgetBI Tool"), flag as price-driven

3. CSM Input

Custom field in SFDC: "Primary Loss Reason" → "Price" vs "Features"

The value: This segmentation lets us route insights to the right team. Pricing team gets one brief, Product team gets another—same pipeline, different outputs.
